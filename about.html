About This Book

Advertisement

Christianity and Islam: A Shared Journey Toward Peace is a work of interfaith dialogue and peace studies written by M.J Usman. It explores the shared ethical teachings of the Holy Qur’an and the Holy Bible and demonstrates how Christians and Muslims can cooperate in promoting peace, justice, and the common good while remaining faithful to their own distinct beliefs.

Purpose

The book was written to highlight the moral principles the two traditions hold in common — peace, justice, mercy, compassion, forgiveness, respect for human dignity, and cooperation — without ignoring or minimising the theological differences that remain between them. It is not a work of comparative theology intended to determine which religion is correct.

How to read it

Readers are encouraged to approach the book with openness, humility, and a sincere desire to understand. Each chapter presents passages from both scriptures, shared principles, a reflection, and a conclusion. You can read it in order from the table of contents or jump to any chapter that interests you.

About the author

M.J Usman is the author and publisher of this work through M.J Usman Publishing, Kano State, Nigeria. The publisher is committed to producing educational and scholarly works that promote knowledge, ethical leadership, mutual understanding, and peaceful coexistence.

Who this is for

The book is intended for students, educators, religious leaders, policymakers, researchers, and any reader who desires stronger relationships between Christians and Muslims.
