About this book

This book highlights the moral teachings that Christianity and Islam hold in common while honestly acknowledging the genuine theological differences between the two faiths. It does not attempt to merge the religions or decide which is correct. Instead, it shows that Christians and Muslims can walk side by side in promoting peace, justice, compassion, and respect while remaining faithful to their own traditions.

Drawing directly on the Holy Qur’an and the Holy Bible, each chapter examines a theme — the sanctity of human life, freedom of worship, respectful dialogue, reconciliation, family relationships, service to society, and the responsibility of young people to become peacemakers.
