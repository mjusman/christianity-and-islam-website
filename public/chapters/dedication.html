Dedication

Advertisement

In the Name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.

This book is first dedicated to Almighty God, the Creator of the heavens and the earth, from whom all wisdom, justice, mercy, and peace ultimately flow. It is dedicated to every Christian and every Muslim who believes that respect is stronger than hatred, that dialogue is wiser than violence, and that peace is more enduring than conflict.

It is dedicated to religious leaders who faithfully guide their communities toward compassion and justice; to teachers who cultivate understanding; to parents who raise children to value honesty and kindness; to students who seek knowledge with humility; and to peacebuilders who work tirelessly to heal divided communities.

May this book encourage every reader to become a bridge between people, a voice for justice, and a servant of peace.
