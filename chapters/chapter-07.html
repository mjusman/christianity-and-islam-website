Chapter 7: Justice Through Law

Advertisement

Introduction

A peaceful society cannot exist without justice, and justice cannot endure without the fair and impartial application of the law. Throughout history, societies have suffered when individuals or groups have taken the law into their own hands through revenge, mob violence, or vigilantism. Such actions often lead to further injustice, the punishment of innocent people, and the breakdown of public order.

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible both teach that justice should be administered with fairness, truthfulness, and respect for due process. They discourage personal vengeance and encourage believers to seek lawful and righteous means of resolving disputes.

This chapter explores how both Islam and Christianity promote justice through lawful authority while rejecting mob justice, collective punishment, and unlawful retaliation.

Justice Through Law in the Holy Qur'an

1. Judge with Justice

Surah An-Nisa' (4:58)

Allah says: "Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due, and when you judge between people, judge with justice..."

This verse establishes that those entrusted with authority—including judges and leaders—must decide cases fairly and honestly.

2. Verify Information Before Acting

Surah Al-Hujurat (49:6)

Allah says: "O you who believe! If a wrongdoer brings you news, verify it, lest you harm people out of ignorance and then become regretful for what you have done."

Justice requires careful investigation. Decisions should never be based on rumors, assumptions, or false accusations.

3. Stand Firm for Justice

Surah An-Nisa' (4:135)

Allah commands believers to uphold justice even when it is against themselves, their families, or their own interests.

True justice requires honesty and impartiality.

4. No One Bears Another's Burden

Surah Al-An'am (6:164)

Allah says: "...No bearer of burdens shall bear the burden of another."

Each person is individually responsible for their own actions. Collective punishment and blaming innocent people for another person's wrongdoing are contrary to this principle.

Justice Through Law in the Holy Bible

1. Do Not Show Partiality

Deuteronomy 16:19-20

The Scriptures instruct judges: "Do not pervert justice or show partiality... Follow justice and justice alone..."

Justice must never be influenced by wealth, power, favoritism, or corruption.

2. Hear Both Sides

Proverbs 18:17

The Bible teaches: "The first to present his case seems right, until another comes forward and questions him."

This proverb highlights the importance of hearing evidence carefully before reaching a judgment.

3. Respect Governing Authorities

Romans 13:1-4

The Apostle Paul teaches that governing authorities have responsibilities to maintain order and administer justice for the benefit of society. This passage encourages respect for lawful authority while recognizing that those in authority are accountable for carrying out justice responsibly.

4. Reject Personal Revenge

Romans 12:19

Paul writes: "Do not take revenge, my dear friends, but leave room for God's justice..."

Believers are discouraged from taking personal vengeance and encouraged to seek justice through righteous and lawful means.

Rejecting Mob Justice and Vigilantism

One of the greatest threats to peace is the practice commonly known as mob justice or jungle justice, where groups punish suspected offenders without lawful investigation or trial.

Such actions often result in: The punishment of innocent people.

 Escalating violence.

 Fear within communities.

 Disregard for the rule of law.

 Cycles of revenge and retaliation.

 Both the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible encourage believers to reject such practices and to uphold justice through lawful processes.

The Importance of Evidence

Justice depends upon truth.

Before any accusation is accepted or judgment is made, there should be: Careful investigation.

 Reliable evidence.

 Honest witnesses.

 Fair hearing.

 Opportunity for defense.

 Impartial judgment.

 False accusations can destroy lives, families, and communities. Both scriptures warn against bearing false witness and spreading unverified claims.

The Role of Courts and Public Institutions

Courts, judges, law enforcement agencies, and other public institutions exist to protect society by administering justice fairly.

Their responsibilities include: Protecting the innocent.

 Investigating crimes impartially.

 Respecting legal rights.

 Preventing corruption.

 Applying the law equally.

 Maintaining public order.

 When these institutions operate with integrity, they strengthen public confidence and reduce the likelihood of violence.

The Responsibility of Religious Communities

Religious leaders and communities also have an important role in promoting lawful justice by: Teaching respect for the rule of law.

 Encouraging peaceful dispute resolution.

 Discouraging revenge and vigilantism.

 Promoting honesty in testimony.

 Supporting reconciliation where appropriate.

 Comforting victims of injustice.

 Their moral guidance can help build societies in which justice and compassion work together.

Justice and Mercy

While justice ensures accountability, mercy opens the door to reconciliation when it is appropriate and does not undermine the rights of victims. The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible both encourage believers to value forgiveness and reconciliation while recognizing that justice remains essential for protecting society.

A healthy legal system seeks both accountability and opportunities for restoration where possible.

Reflection

Communities become safer when disputes are resolved through lawful institutions rather than through anger or revenge.

Every citizen who respects the rule of law contributes to peace. Every leader who judges fairly strengthens public trust. Every witness who speaks truthfully helps protect the innocent.

Justice through law is not merely a legal necessity—it is an expression of moral responsibility and respect for the dignity of every human being.

Conclusion

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible both teach that justice should be pursued through fairness, truth, and lawful authority. They reject false accusations, personal vengeance, and mob justice, emphasizing instead the importance of evidence, impartial judgment, and respect for the rights of all people. Christians and Muslims can work together to support legal systems that protect the innocent, hold wrongdoers accountable, and preserve public peace. When justice is administered through lawful means, society is strengthened, trust is restored, and conflicts are less likely to lead to violence. "...When you judge between people, judge with justice."

— Qur'an 4:58 "Follow justice and justice alone..."

— Deuteronomy 16:20 A society governed by justice through law is one in which every person can live with greater security, dignity, and hope.
