Chapter 6: Justice Without Oppression

Advertisement

Introduction

Justice is one of the strongest pillars of a peaceful and prosperous society. Wherever justice is upheld, people are more likely to live in security, trust one another, and resolve disputes peacefully. Conversely, oppression, corruption, discrimination, and abuse of power often become the seeds of conflict, resentment, and violence.

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible consistently teach that justice is not optional but a divine command. Believers are instructed to act fairly, defend the rights of others, and refuse to allow hatred, personal interests, or prejudice to influence their judgment.

This chapter explores how both Islam and Christianity call their followers to establish justice while rejecting every form of oppression. It demonstrates that lasting peace can only flourish where justice is practiced with integrity, compassion, and respect for human dignity.

Justice in the Holy Qur'an

1. Stand Firm for Justice

Surah An-Nisa' (4:135)

Allah says: "O you who believe! Stand firmly for justice as witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves, your parents, or your close relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. So do not follow your desires, lest you fail to be just..."

This verse teaches that justice must never be influenced by family ties, wealth, social status, or personal interest. True justice requires honesty and impartiality.

2. Do Not Let Hatred Lead to Injustice

Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:8)

Allah says: "Do not let the hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to righteousness."

Even during disagreement or conflict, Muslims are commanded to uphold justice. Personal dislike or religious differences must never become an excuse for unfair treatment.

3. Allah Commands Justice

Surah An-Nahl (16:90)

Allah says: "Indeed, Allah commands justice, excellence, and generosity toward relatives, and He forbids immorality, wrongdoing, and oppression."

This verse is widely regarded as one of the Qur'an's clearest summaries of righteous conduct. Justice and kindness are presented as inseparable qualities of a faithful life.

4. Justice as a Trust

Surah An-Nisa' (4:58)

Allah commands believers to return trusts to those entitled to them and to judge fairly when making decisions between people.

Justice is therefore both a moral obligation and a sacred trust.

Justice in the Holy Bible

1. Act Justly

Micah 6:8

The prophet Micah declares: "He has shown you, O man, what is good. And what does the Lord require of you? To act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God."

Justice is presented as one of God's fundamental expectations for every believer.

2. Defend the Vulnerable

Isaiah 1:17

The prophet Isaiah teaches: "Learn to do good; seek justice. Correct oppression. Defend the fatherless. Plead the cause of the widow."

The Bible repeatedly emphasizes that justice includes protecting those who are most vulnerable.

3. Do Not Show Partiality

James 2:1-9

The Apostle James warns believers not to treat the rich more favorably than the poor.

Justice requires equality before God and fairness toward every person.

4. The Greatest Commandments

Matthew 22:37-40

Jesus teaches that love for God and love for one's neighbor summarize God's law.

True justice is inseparable from genuine love and concern for others.

Rejecting Every Form of Oppression

Both the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible condemn oppression in all its forms.

Oppression may include: Abuse of political power.

 Religious persecution.

 Corruption.

 Bribery.

 Exploitation of workers.

 Human trafficking.

 Racism and ethnic discrimination.

 Domestic abuse.

 Denial of basic human rights.

 Economic exploitation.

 Oppression harms not only its victims but also the moral fabric of society.

Justice for All People

Justice must never depend upon: Religion.

 Ethnicity.

 Nationality.

 Language.

 Gender.

 Wealth.

 Social status.

 Political affiliation.

 Every individual deserves equal treatment before the law and equal respect for their human dignity.

When justice is applied consistently, trust grows between communities and peace becomes more secure.

The Responsibility of Leaders

Political leaders, judges, law enforcement officers, religious leaders, educators, and community leaders bear a special responsibility to administer justice honestly.

Their duties include: Protecting the weak.

 Preventing corruption.

 Applying laws fairly.

 Rejecting favoritism.

 Respecting human rights.

 Serving the public with integrity.

 Leadership is a trust that should always be exercised for the benefit of society rather than for personal gain.

The Responsibility of Every Citizen

Justice is not the responsibility of leaders alone.

Every individual contributes to a just society by: Speaking truthfully.

 Keeping promises.

 Respecting the rights of others.

 Rejecting bribery and corruption.

 Reporting injustice through lawful means.

 Treating neighbors fairly.

 Showing honesty in business and personal relationships.

 A peaceful society is built when ordinary people choose integrity in their daily lives.

Justice as the Foundation of Peace

Peace cannot exist where injustice is allowed to flourish.

Communities affected by corruption, discrimination, and oppression often experience division, violence, and mistrust. By contrast, societies that uphold justice create conditions in which people can live together with confidence and mutual respect.

Christians and Muslims therefore share a responsibility to support institutions and practices that protect fairness, accountability, and the equal dignity of every person.

Reflection

History repeatedly demonstrates that injustice eventually leads to conflict, while justice creates opportunities for reconciliation and lasting peace. Every decision made with honesty, every act of fairness, and every effort to defend the rights of another person strengthens the moral foundation of society.

Justice is not merely a legal principle—it is an expression of faith, obedience to God, and love for one's neighbor.

Conclusion

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible both call believers to uphold justice without prejudice and to reject every form of oppression. Justice is not limited to courtrooms or governments; it begins in the hearts and actions of individuals who choose fairness over favoritism and truth over deception. When Christians and Muslims commit themselves to justice, they become partners in building societies where every person is treated with dignity, every right is respected, and peace is able to flourish.

"Be just; that is nearer to righteousness."

— Qur'an 5:8 "Act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God."

— Micah 6:8 Justice without oppression is one of the greatest gifts that believers can offer to their communities, their nations, and the world.
