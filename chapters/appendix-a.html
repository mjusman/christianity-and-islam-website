Appendix A: Shared Ethical Teachings of the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible

Introduction

The purpose of this appendix is to provide readers with a concise comparison of ethical teachings found in the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible. Throughout this book, we have seen that Christianity and Islam differ in important theological beliefs and religious doctrines. These differences should be acknowledged honestly and respectfully.

At the same time, both scriptures contain many moral teachings that encourage believers to live with justice, compassion, mercy, honesty, and respect for human dignity. These shared ethical principles provide opportunities for Christians and Muslims to cooperate in promoting peace and serving the common good without compromising their own religious convictions.

The passages listed below are representative examples rather than an exhaustive collection. Readers are encouraged to study these passages within their broader scriptural context and, where appropriate, consult reliable commentaries and qualified scholars from their own religious traditions.

1. Peace

Holy Qur'an

10:25 — Allah invites humanity to the Home of Peace.

 8:61 — Accept peace when the other party sincerely inclines toward it.  49:9–10 — Reconcile believers who are in conflict.

 41:34 — Repel evil with what is better.



Holy Bible

Matthew 5:9 — "Blessed are the peacemakers."

 Romans 12:18 — Live at peace with everyone whenever possible.

 Psalm 34:14 — Seek peace and pursue it.

 Hebrews 12:14 — Pursue peace with everyone.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Peace should be actively pursued through justice, reconciliation, and wise conduct.

2. Sanctity of Human Life

Holy Qur'an

5:32 — The unjust killing of one innocent person is compared to  killing all humanity, while saving one life is compared to saving all humanity.

17:33 — Do not take innocent life except according to justice.



Holy Bible

Exodus 20:13 — "You shall not murder."

 Genesis 9:6 — Human life possesses special value because humanity is  made in God's image.

Genesis 1:26–27 — Humanity is created in the image of God.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Human life is sacred and must be protected.

3. Justice

Holy Qur'an

4:135 — Stand firmly for justice, even against oneself or one's  relatives.

5:8 — Do not allow hatred to prevent justice.

 16:90 — Allah commands justice, excellence, and generosity.



Holy Bible

Micah 6:8 — Act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with God.

 Isaiah 1:17 — Learn to do good, seek justice, defend the oppressed.  Proverbs 21:3 — Doing justice is pleasing to God.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Justice must never be abandoned because of personal interest, prejudice, or hatred.

4. Mercy and Compassion

Holy Qur'an

21:107 — The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is described as a mercy to the  worlds.

24:22 — Forgive and overlook the faults of others.

 39:53 — Do not despair of Allah's mercy.



Holy Bible

Matthew 5:7 — Blessed are the merciful.

 Luke 6:36 — Be merciful as your Father is merciful.

 Colossians 3:12 — Clothe yourselves with compassion and kindness.  Shared Ethical Principle: Mercy reflects God's character and should be practiced toward others.

5. Forgiveness

Holy Qur'an

42:40 — Whoever forgives and seeks reconciliation will be rewarded  by Allah.

3:134 — Those who restrain anger and forgive are praised by Allah. 

Holy Bible

Matthew 6:14–15 — Forgive others as you seek God's forgiveness.  Ephesians 4:32 — Forgive one another as God forgave you.

 Colossians 3:13 — Bear with one another and forgive.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Forgiveness promotes healing and reconciliation while remaining consistent with justice.

6. Love of Neighbor

Holy Qur'an

2:195 — Allah loves those who do good.

 41:34 — Respond to evil with what is better.

 5:2 — Cooperate in righteousness and piety.



Holy Bible

Mark 12:31 — Love your neighbor as yourself.

 Matthew 22:39 — Love your neighbor.

 1 Corinthians 13:4–7 — Love is patient, kind, and enduring.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Love is expressed through service, kindness, generosity, and concern for others.

7. Respectful Speech

Holy Qur'an

16:125 — Invite others with wisdom and good instruction.

 6:108 — Do not insult what others worship lest they respond with  hostility.

17:53 — Speak in the best manner.



Holy Bible

Ephesians 4:29 — Speak only words that build others up.

 James 1:19 — Be quick to listen and slow to speak.

 Proverbs 15:1 — A gentle answer turns away wrath.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Words should promote understanding rather than hatred.

8. Religious Freedom and Respect

Holy Qur'an

2:256 — There is no compulsion in religion.

 29:46 — Discuss with the People of the Book in the best manner.  60:8 — Show kindness and justice toward peaceful people of other  faiths.

Holy Bible

Romans 12:18 — Live peacefully with everyone.

 1 Peter 3:15 — Defend your faith with gentleness and respect.

 Matthew 5:9 — Blessed are the peacemakers.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Religious convictions should be expressed with respect rather than coercion or hostility.

9. Care for the Poor and Vulnerable

Holy Qur'an

2:177 — Righteousness includes caring for relatives, orphans, the  needy, and travelers.

9:60 — Zakat supports those in need.

 93:9–10 — Do not oppress the orphan or turn away the needy.



Holy Bible

Matthew 25:35–40 — Serving those in need is service to Christ.

 James 1:27 — Care for orphans and widows.

 Proverbs 19:17 — Kindness to the poor honors God.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Compassion for the vulnerable is a central responsibility of believers.

10. Honesty and Integrity

Holy Qur'an

9:119 — Be with those who are truthful.

 83:1–3 — Condemnation of dishonest trade and cheating.

 2:188 — Do not consume one another's wealth unjustly.



Holy Bible

Proverbs 12:22 — God delights in those who are truthful.

 Ephesians 4:25 — Speak truthfully to one another.

 Luke 16:10 — Faithfulness in small matters reflects integrity.

 Shared Ethical Principle: Honesty strengthens trust within families, communities, and nations.

11. Family Responsibility

Holy Qur'an

30:21 — Marriage is founded upon affection and mercy.

 4:19 — Live with spouses in kindness.

 17:23 — Show kindness to parents.



Holy Bible

Genesis 2:24 — Marriage establishes lifelong unity.

 Ephesians 5:25 — Husbands are called to love their wives.

 Ephesians 6:1–4 — Honor parents and nurture children responsibly.  Shared Ethical Principle: Strong families are essential for peaceful societies.

12. Cooperation for the Common Good

Holy Qur'an

5:2 — Cooperate in righteousness and piety.

 49:13 — Human diversity exists so that people may know one another. 

Holy Bible

Galatians 6:10 — Do good to everyone whenever possible.

 Hebrews 10:24 — Encourage one another toward love and good  works.

Philippians 2:4 — Look to the interests of others as well as your own.  Shared Ethical Principle: Cooperation in serving humanity strengthens peace and benefits society.

Closing Reflection

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible remain distinct sacred scriptures, each interpreted within its own religious tradition. They differ on significant theological matters that should be studied with care and honesty. At the same time, they contain many shared ethical teachings that encourage justice, compassion, mercy, truthfulness, service, and peaceful coexistence. These common moral principles provide a meaningful basis for cooperation between Christians and Muslims in addressing the challenges facing families, communities, and nations.

Recognizing these shared values does not erase theological differences. Rather, it reminds us that people of faith can remain committed to their own beliefs while working together to protect human dignity, relieve suffering, and promote peace for the benefit of all humanity.
