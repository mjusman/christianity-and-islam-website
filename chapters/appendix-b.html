Appendix B: Selected Prayers for Peace from the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible

Introduction

Prayer occupies a central place in both Christianity and Islam. It is through prayer that believers seek God's guidance, mercy, forgiveness, protection, and peace. While the forms of prayer differ between the two faiths, both the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible encourage believers to turn to God in times of joy, sorrow, conflict, and uncertainty.

This appendix brings together selected prayers and passages related to peace from the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible. They are presented to illustrate the shared desire of believers to seek God's help in living justly, loving others, and promoting peace.

These prayers are not intended to replace the established prayers or liturgies of either religion. Rather, they are examples drawn from each tradition that encourage reflection on peace, mercy, and reconciliation.

Section I: Selected Prayers from the Holy Qur'an

1. Prayer for Guidance

Surah Al-Fatihah (1:5-7)

"You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the Straight Path—the path of those upon whom You have bestowed Your grace, not of those who have earned Your anger nor of those who have gone astray."

Theme: Seeking God's guidance toward righteousness and truth.

2. Prayer for Peaceful Hearts

Surah Al-Hashr (59:10)

"Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and do not place in our hearts any resentment toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Most Kind, Most Merciful."

Theme: Forgiveness, unity, and freedom from hatred.

3. Prayer for Mercy

Surah Al-A'raf (7:23)

"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."

Theme: Repentance and divine mercy.

4. Prayer for Patience and Steadfastness

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:250)

"Our Lord, pour upon us patience, make our steps firm, and grant us victory over wrongdoing."

Theme: Courage, perseverance, and trust in God.

5. Prayer for Wisdom

Surah Ta-Ha (20:25-28)

The prayer of Prophet Moses (Mūsā): "My Lord, expand my breast, ease my task for me, untie the knot from my tongue, so that they may understand my speech."

Theme: Wisdom, clarity, and effective communication.

6. Prayer for Family

Surah Al-Furqan (25:74)

"Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses and our children comfort to our eyes and make us leaders for the righteous."

Theme: Peace within families and righteous leadership.

7. Prayer for Goodness in This Life and the Next

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:201)

"Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."

Theme: Well-being, hope, and God's protection.

8. Prayer for Unity

Surah Aal 'Imran (3:8)

"Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. Indeed, You are the Bestower."

Theme: Steadfast faith and divine mercy.

Section II: Selected Prayers from the Holy Bible

1. The Lord's Prayer

Matthew 6:9-13

Jesus teaches His disciples to pray: "Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us today our daily bread. Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil."

Theme: Worship, dependence on God, forgiveness, and righteous living.

2. Prayer for Peace

Psalm 122:6-8

"Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: 'May those who love you be secure... May there be peace within your walls and security within your citadels.'"

Theme: Peace for communities and nations.

3. Prayer for God's Guidance

Psalm 25:4-5

"Show me your ways, Lord, teach me your paths. Guide me in your truth and teach me."

Theme: Seeking divine direction and wisdom.

4. Prayer for a Pure Heart

Psalm 51:10

"Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me."

Theme: Spiritual renewal and inner peace.

5. Prayer for Wisdom

James 1:5

"If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives generously to all without finding fault."

Theme: Seeking God's wisdom.

6. Prayer Against Anxiety

Philippians 4:6-7

"Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God... will guard your hearts and your minds."

Theme: Trust, peace, and confidence in God.

7. Prayer for Unity

John 17:20-23

Jesus prays for His followers: "...that they may all be one..."

Theme: Unity among believers.

8. Blessing of Peace

Numbers 6:24-26

"The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make His face shine upon you and be gracious to you; the Lord turn His face toward you and give you peace."

Theme: God's blessing, protection, and peace.

Shared Themes in Prayer

Although Christianity and Islam differ in their forms of worship and theology, the prayers presented above reveal several shared themes. Both traditions encourage believers to pray for: Peace.

 Guidance.

 Mercy.

 Forgiveness.

 Wisdom.

 Patience.

 Justice.

 Family well-being.

 Protection from evil.

 Faithfulness to God.

 These common concerns reflect humanity's universal dependence upon God's guidance and compassion.

Praying for Peace in Daily Life

Both Christians and Muslims are encouraged not only to pray for peace but also to become instruments of peace through their actions.

Prayer should inspire believers to: Speak truthfully.

 Forgive others.

 Care for those in need.

 Reject hatred.

 Defend justice.

 Help reconcile conflicts.

 Treat neighbors with kindness.

 Respect the dignity of every human being.

 Prayer and righteous action belong together.

Reflection

Prayer reminds believers that lasting peace ultimately comes from God. While human effort is necessary to resolve conflicts and build just societies, true peace also requires humble hearts that seek divine guidance, mercy, and wisdom.

The Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible both encourage believers to approach God with sincerity, trusting that He hears the prayers of those who seek Him faithfully.

Closing Meditation

Whether spoken in Arabic, Hebrew, Greek, English, or any other language, sincere prayers for peace express one of humanity's deepest hopes: that justice will prevail, mercy will triumph over hatred, and people will live together with dignity and compassion.

May the prayers found in the Holy Qur'an and the Holy Bible continue to inspire believers to become peacemakers in their homes, their places of worship, their communities, and the wider world.

"Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter..."

— Holy Qur'an 2:201 "Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God."

— Matthew 5:9 May Almighty God guide all people toward justice, wisdom, mercy, and peace.

Amen.

Āmīn.
